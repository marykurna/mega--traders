FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Our support team replies in under a minute, 24/7.

Do I need to deposit money to get started?

No. Every account comes with a fully funded demo environment using live market data. You can explore the platform, build strategies and backtest them before committing a single dollar.

What markets can I trade?

Crypto pairs, US and EU equities, forex, indices and commodities — all from one margin balance. Over 4,000 instruments are available on the Pro plan and above.

How are my funds protected?

Client funds are held in segregated accounts with tier-1 banks, separate from company capital. Eligible accounts are covered by the investor compensation scheme up to $50,000.

What are the actual fees?

There are no custody fees, no inactivity fees and no deposit fees. You pay the spread, plus a small commission on equities. Withdrawals above $50 are free.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Plans are billed monthly and you can cancel from your dashboard in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the current billing period — no lock-in, no exit fees.

Do you offer an API?

REST and FIX APIs are available on Pro and Institution plans, with WebSocket streaming for real-time market data. Full documentation is public and includes SDKs for Python, Node and Go.
